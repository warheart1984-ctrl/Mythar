# Multi-Worldline Interference — Entanglement Waves on the Boundary

> Show that interactions in 4D create structured, wave-like patterns in the boundary entanglement field.

## Setup

**Two worldlines in the bulk:**
- W1: particle A moving along one path
- W2: particle B moving along another path

**They interact** in a region of spacetime (e.g. via a simple potential or collision).

## Pipeline

### Bulk

Simulate both worldlines and their interaction (even a toy "they get close → interaction strength spikes" is enough).

### Boundary

Project both worldlines to the boundary. When they interact in 4D, increase:
- Local ρ at overlapping boundary regions
- Entanglement weights wᵢⱼ in those regions

## Resulting Pattern

You should see:
- Interference-like structures in ρ and wᵢⱼ
- Entanglement "ripples" propagating across the boundary graph

**Visually:**
- Bright overlapping trails
- Wavefronts of entanglement spreading from the interaction region
- Curvature K responding to those gradients

That's your first entanglement wave experiment.
